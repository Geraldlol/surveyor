# Surveyor

## 0.1.0

- World map layer: on a zone map, outlines every area with an exploration bit along its chunk
  boundary; undiscovered areas gold, discovered areas green. On a continent map, outlines the
  top-level zones.
- Area names inside each outline (toggle with `/survey names`).
- `/survey on | off | names | xp | all | status`; `xp` (default) draws only the areas that pay
  discovery XP.
- Border data generated offline from Forever 1.60.1.70450.
- Initial scaffold.
